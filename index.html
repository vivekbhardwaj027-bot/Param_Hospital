About Us

Healing with
Humanitarian Spirit

Param Hospital, a proud unit of D and P Group, is situated on Hisar Road, Meham (Rohtak). We are committed to delivering compassionate, ethical, and affordable healthcare to every patient who walks through our doors.

From a state-of-the-art ICU and HDU to digital X-Ray, laboratory diagnostics and 24×7 emergency services — we combine modern medical infrastructure with genuine human care.

Critical Care

ICU & HDU Services

State-of-the-art intensive care with round-the-clock monitoring and support.

ICU Visiting Hours

10:30 AM – 11:30 AM
4:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Rules

  • Eatables from outside are not allowed
  • Restricted entry — only permitted attendants

ICU Admission Criteria

  • Dyspnea with RR > 30
  • SpO₂ < 90
  • BP < 100/70
  • Snake bite, drowning, poisoning, hanging etc.
  • Severe chest pain
  • Cardiac insufficiency
  • Severe jaundice
  • Septicemia
  • Multi-organ failure
  • Exceptionally high BP > 150/100
  • Cerebral vascular accident
  • Unconsciousness
  • Severe peritonitis
  • Hemorrhagic shock
  • Multiple fractures

ICU Shifting Criteria

  • Improvement in clinical status
  • Stable patient
  • No ventilator requirement
  • Stable oxygen level
  • Stable blood pressure
  • Controlled bleeding
  • Stable neurological condition
  • Transfer to specialty center if needed

Critical Alert Findings

The following findings are reported immediately to the clinician:

  • Fractures with or without displacement
  • Pneumoperitoneum
  • Subcutaneous emphysema
  • Pneumomediastinum
  • Foreign bodies in airway
  • Congestive heart failure
  • Pneumothorax
  • Lung collapse
  • Pleural effusion
  • Intestinal obstruction
  • Cervical spine fractures
  • Vertebral collapse
